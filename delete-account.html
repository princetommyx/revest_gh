Account Deletion Request

If you would like to delete your Revest account and all associated data, you have two options:

Option 1: Delete from within the app

  1. Open the Revest app and log in to your account.
  2. Go to your Profile or Settings screen.
  3. Look for the Delete Account option and follow the prompts.

Option 2: Submit a deletion request

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot log in, please send an email to request account deletion. Your data will be deleted within 30 days of your request.
