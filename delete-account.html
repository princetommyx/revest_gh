Account Deletion Request

App Name: Revesta

If you would like to delete or deactivate your Revesta account and associated data, please follow the steps below.

Option 1: Delete or Deactivate immediately within the app

  1. Open the Revesta app and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to your Profile page.
  3. Look for the Delete Account or Deactivate Account option.
  4. Immediately after clicking, your account will be processed for deletion or deactivation instantly.

Option 2: Request permanent data deletion via Email

If you have already deleted or deactivated your account in the app but want to ensure that all of your data has been permanently and thoroughly wiped from our database, or if you no longer have the app installed, please send an email to request permanent data deletion.

Email: (Insert your support email here)

Subject: Permanent Data Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your Revesta account in the body of the email.

Data Deletion and Retention Policy

Types of Data Deleted

  • Personal information (Name, Email Address, Phone Number)
  • Authentication data and OAuth tokens
  • Profile photos and user-generated content
  • App activity and usage history

Types of Data Kept

  • We do not retain any personally identifiable information after a permanent deletion request is processed.
  • Anonymous, aggregated analytics data (which cannot be traced back to you) may be kept indefinitely for business reporting.

Retention Period

In-app deletions take effect immediately. For email requests, we will process your request and permanently delete your data within 30 days of receiving your email.
