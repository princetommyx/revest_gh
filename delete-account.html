Account Deletion Request

If you would like to delete or deactivate your Revesta account, you have two options:

Option 1: Delete or Deactivate immediately within the app

  1. Open the Revesta app and log in to your account.
  2. Navigate to your Profile page.
  3. Look for the Delete Account or Deactivate Account option.
  4. Immediately after clicking, your account will be processed for deletion or deactivation instantly.

Option 2: Request permanent data deletion

If you have already deleted or deactivated your account in the app but want to ensure that all of your data has been permanently and thoroughly wiped from our database, or if you no longer have the app installed, please send an email to request permanent data deletion.

Subject: Permanent Data Deletion Request

Please include the email address associated with your Revesta account in the body of the email. We will process your request and confirm permanent deletion within 30 days.
